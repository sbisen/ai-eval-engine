Domain accuracy of generated questions — with vs without domain context

FinanceBench golden sets · we test the questions, not the answers

Metric: graded question domain accuracy = mean of two axes an LLM-as-judge (holding the raw FinanceBench domain JSON) scores per generated question — domain relevance (is it a real 10-K finance question) and groundedness (does it pin a specific company/period/figure verifiable against the raw data). We test the questions, not answers; compliance/refusal probes excluded. With context (generator had the raw domain info + runbook): mean 93% — relevant and grounded. Without context (generator had neither): mean 51% — the questions are valid finance questions (relevance credit) but company-less, so they verify against no specific filing (low groundedness). The moral: domain context lets the generator write questions that are not just on-topic but actually gradeable against the domain.
