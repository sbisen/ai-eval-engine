Per-question domain relatedness — with vs without domain context

FinanceBench golden sets · distribution over the questions (not answers)

Each generated question is scored by its domain relatedness — the max TF-IDF cosine similarity of the question text to the closest single real FinanceBench filing record (over all question + answer + evidence text). Objective, offline, hand-reproducible — not a soft judge confidence. Box = IQR, line = median, hollow dot = mean, faint dots = individual questions. With context: median 0.263, mean 0.260 (n=36). Without context: median 0.176, mean 0.192 (n=50) — roughly a 49% higher median. Both are genuinely finance questions, so the distributions still overlap; the with-context set lands closer to real filing records because its questions name specific companies, line items, and periods drawn from the domain.
