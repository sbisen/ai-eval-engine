Question topics in domain space — with vs without domain context

FinanceBench golden sets · bubbles = NMF topics over the questions (not answers)

Two golden sets, two colors. Each bubble is a question topic (NMF over the raw question text). x = mean domain relatedness (max TF-IDF cosine to the closest real FinanceBench filing record); y = mean domain specificity (anchors pinned /5: company, period, statement, line item, verifiable answer); size = number of questions. We test the questions, not answers. With context topics sit up and to the right — specific and close to real filings — and the set also spans a lower-left band of safety / refusal topics the without-context generator never produced. Without-context topics line the bottom: valid finance topics, but generic, so low specificity. Same axes, same metric — domain context moves whole topics up and to the right, and adds a safety dimension that otherwise does not exist.
